Operational rules for Certificate Issuers and instructions for Public Verifiers.

CRITICAL OPERATIONAL RULE FOR ISSUERS

When you upload certificate files in the Certificate Locker (/locker), the Recipient Name is automatically extracted from the uploaded filename.

Example Filename Formatting:

✔ John_Doe_BSc_Computer_Science.pdf → Recipient: John_Doe_BSc_Computer_Science

✖ scan_00129_final.pdf → Recipient: scan_00129_final

Always rename your certificate files to include the student or recipient's full name prior to dragging them into the locker.

1. Guide for Certificate Issuers

100% Client-Side Privacy

Hashes are calculated inside your browser using W3C SubtleCrypto SHA-512. Original PDF or image documents are never transmitted over the network or stored on servers.

Solidity Smart Contract

Published digests are stored in smart contract Locker.sol on Ethereum Sepolia Testnet, binding your wallet address and organization metadata to the digest.

Zero-RPC History Log

All your issued certificates are indexed locally in the database. Access /history anytime to view records instantly without making slow blockchain network calls.

On-Chain Revocation

If a certificate was issued erroneously or needs to be revoked, click Revoke On-Chain in /history to execute updateData() on Ethereum.

2. Guide for Public Verifiers

How to Audit a Certificate

Visit the Public Audit Portal at /verify. Drag and drop the candidate certificate file (PDF, PNG, JPG) or paste its 128-character SHA-512 digest.

Sensitivity to Edits & Tampering

SHA-512 hashing is strictly deterministic. If someone modifies even 1 character, name, date, or pixel in a PDF, the resulting hash will change completely, failing verification.
