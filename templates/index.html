Platform Architecture

How Voltus Anchors Certificates

01

Browser Digest Computation

Documents are hashed locally using W3C standard SubtleCrypto SHA-512. Files never leave the browser, preserving 100% data privacy.

02

Smart Contract Commit

The SHA-512 digest and issuer metadata are written to Solidity smart contract Locker.sol on Ethereum Sepolia.

03

Zero-Friction Audit

Employers and institutions verify certificate authenticity instantly on the public audit portal without accounts or fees.
